# SOS Fixture demo photos

These seven local JPEGs are Fixture clothing only. They are never loaded from Supabase or copied from a private closet. `tops.jpg` and `dress.jpg` reuse public demo image IDs already present in `src/constants/index.js`; the other five are free Unsplash photos. The [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) permits reuse of these photos. These files are bundled locally so Fixture can be tested without external image requests.
